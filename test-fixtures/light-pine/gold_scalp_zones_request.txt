Build the first stage of a gold scalping overlay (XAUUSD, 5m chart) from a
top-down entry model. On the 15m higher timeframe, detect a sell-side liquidity
sweep that closes back above the prior three-bar low, then a bullish break of
structure: a close above the prior three-bar high. The order block is the last
down-close 15m bar before that expansion; draw it as a time-anchored zone. On
the chart timeframe, find a bearish fair value gap (a candle high below the low
two candles earlier) and mark it as an inverse FVG once a close takes its top,
because it then acts as support for the long entry. Label each zone with its
role, anchor both by absolute time, use completed higher-timeframe bars only,
and do not add entries, stops, or trade execution yet.
