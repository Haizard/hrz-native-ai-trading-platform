The footprint ladder, drawn the way the shell draws it

A real Mode::Footprint scene from chart_engine.wasm, rendered with the same rules as drawFootprintGrid in app.js. Every coordinate and every string below comes from the engine; the drawing only picks colours and calls fillText.

The three things to look at: every cell with volume is filled, tinted by which side won the level; the value area is banded per column, so it is a band inside each ladder rather than a stripe across all of them; and the pair reads as bid x ask, centred, rather than as two numbers pushed into the two halves of the cell.

    An imbalance is outlined, and its outline is the data. A diagonal imbalance is this level measured against the level below it — not simply which of this cell's two numbers is larger — which is why a bid-heavy cell can still be flagged as a buy imbalance. The ratio sets how saturated the outline is and stacked sets how thick, so both numbers the detector produces are load-bearing rather than carried and unused.

    A pair that does not fit in its cell is not drawn. A truncated number is a wrong number, and a ladder of wrong numbers is worse than a ladder of colours — so below roughly 54px a column is a heat map, which is the honest thing for it to be. Narrow the window and that is what you should see.
