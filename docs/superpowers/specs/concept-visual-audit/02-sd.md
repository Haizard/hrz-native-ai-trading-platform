# Pass 2 — Supply & Demand (4 rows)

Judged 2026-10-29 against `main`. The machine-readable verdicts are the 4 rows of
[`ledger/sd.json`](../../../../reports/concept-visual-audit/ledger/sd.json);
`node tools/concept_visual_audit.mjs check --family sd` reports **4 rows, 0 incomplete**.

**Headline: the family is split down the middle between two incompatible theories of what
a zone is, and the picture shows neither.** One of the four concepts is computed from a
supply/demand detector that already gets the reference's rule right — origin at the base,
mitigation tracked, death candle recorded — and the event boundary throws all of that away.
The other three are computed from an S/R *touch-cluster* detector the reference set names as
the opposite school ("An S/R zone earns credibility through repeated touches; a supply or
demand zone is considered strongest before it is touched at all"). The catalogue's own
descriptions speak the first school's language; the numbers underneath come from the second.

**The plan's test question — does pass 1's vocabulary generalise? — answered yes.** Three of
the four rows ask for shapes pass 1 already settled (`lifecycle_box` × 3) and the fourth
claims `readout`, which until now was a candidate on pass 11's say-so and is now defined. No
fifth kind and no new geometry: the ceiling pass 1 measured is still the ceiling.

## What the reference set says

Two LuxAlgo Library concept pages carry this family, and they agree with each other and with
the description our own panel gives the group ("The untested zones above and
below price that a move started from.",
[concept_taxonomy.js:38](../../../../frontend/app/concept_taxonomy.js#L38)):

1. **A zone is an origin, not a level price keeps visiting.** "A demand zone is a small
   consolidation, called a base, that price left with an impulsive rally"; "The zone is found
   by working backward from an impulsive move to the consolidation that launched it"
   ([Supply & Demand Zones](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/supply-and-demand-zones/)).
2. **Its two edges have names and jobs.** "Place the distal line at the base's far extreme
   (lowest wick of a demand base, highest wick of a supply base) and the proximal line at the
   near edge, commonly the candle bodies" — and "the proximal line serves as the entry trigger
   and the distal line anchors the stop"
   ([Zone Scoring & Refinement](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/zone-scoring-and-refinement/)).
   One convention, wicks or bodies, kept constant "so stop distances stay comparable".
3. **It lives until consumed, and the grade is a filter, not a figure.** "zones are marked
   consumed once price trades cleanly through them, and zone scoring and refinement frameworks
   rank the survivors by freshness, departure strength, and higher-timeframe alignment"; "To
   declutter automated charts, where strength classifiers render or alert on only the top-graded
   zones". Nothing in either page draws a score as a chart object.
4. **Freshness outranks touches — in this school.** "Zone scoring inverts that premise: each
   touch consumes the resting orders behind the zone, so freshness outranks touch count."

That fourth sentence is the one this pass measures ourselves against, because our zone-strength
rule does the opposite, and does it in the family the sentence is about.

## What we draw today

**1. The zone model is right, and the event boundary loses it.** `sd.zone_tested` runs on
[regions::detect_zones](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/regions.rs#L241), which is a
textbook supply/demand detector: walk back from a structure break over the impulse run, take
the run of at most `max_origin_candles` (3) opposite-coloured candles before it as the base,
require that base to be a *pause* (`max_origin_ratio: Some(0.5)` — "an origin is a pause, and
a 'zone' drawn across ten candles is the whole preceding range"), band it from the base's low
to its high, and then track `mitigated` (0.0-1.0) and `mitigated_at`, with `to` documented as
"the last candle's close time. A zone that stopped at the break would be a historical
annotation; the point of the drawing is that it is still in play"
([regions.rs:253](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/regions.rs#L253)). That is the
reference's shape, computed, in Rust, today. `as_concept_event` keeps `zone_low`, `zone_high`
and `formed_at` and drops `name`, `from`, `to`, `mitigated` and `mitigated_at`
([concepts.rs:2447](../../../../crates/market-data/src/concepts.rs#L2447)) — so the zone's
*side* ("demand"/"supply", which that module calls its colour key and its label) and its whole
lifecycle never reach the browser. What is left draws as a 1-4 bar band ending at the test bar
(the nine measured spans are 1, 1, 2, 2, 2, 3, 3, 3, 4 bars; heights 79-154 USD).

**2. The other three read a different detector, and only its first result.**
`sd.proximal_distal`, `sd.zone_compression` and `sd.zone_strength` all come out of
`detect_structure_levels`, which clusters *market-structure swings* within 0.4% with at least
2 touches, keeps the 6 best by touch count, and then reads `zones.first()`
([events.rs:2004-2007](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/events.rs#L2004),
[sr_zones.rs:89](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/sr_zones.rs#L89)) — the touch-based
school the reference page explicitly contrasts with this one. On the measured window that is
**one zone**, 80,026.57-80,717.59: 691 USD tall, i.e. between 1.84 and 10.35 times the 14-bar
average range across all 588 measurable bars, where the nine real supply/demand zones in the
same window are 79-154 USD tall — 4.5 to 8.7 times narrower. The two detectors do not disagree
at the margin; they are an order of magnitude apart about what a zone is.

**3. Because it is one zone re-emitted every bar, `sd.proximal_distal` draws two grey
smudges.** 50 events, spans 420 to 469 bars, `direction: neutral` on all 50, and
`price_level` at one of exactly two prices — 80,717.59 (31 events) and 80,026.57 (19) —
because "proximal" is whichever edge the current close happens to be nearer, so it flips when
price crosses the zone. The router sends the concept to `segment` (it is not in
`CONCEPT_ZONE_IDS`), so 50 overlapping horizontal lines paint as the two bars in
[pass2-chart.png](evidence/pass2-chart.png). The reference's two named edges are both there,
but nothing on the chart says which is the entry and which is the invalidation — which is the
only reason to have two.

**4. The inverted pair is a bug, not a style.** `push`'s slots are
`(kind, side, price, low, high, formed_at)`, and the call is
`push(EventKind::ProximalDistal, None, proximal, Some(distal), Some(proximal), …)`
([events.rs:2016](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/events.rs#L2016)) — so `distal` goes
into `zone_low` and `proximal` into `zone_high`. When the close is below the zone that is
backwards, and 19 of the 50 measured events carry `zone_low: 80717.59 > zone_high: 80026.57`.
Every consumer that trusts the field names is then wrong: the card computes
`Math.max(2, bottom - top)` for its rectangle and draws a 2px sliver
([app.js:14786](../../../../frontend/app/app.js#L14786)).

**5. `sd.zone_compression` cannot reach its own threshold.** The rule is
`height < atr14 * 0.5` against the same single S/R zone, which measures 1.84-10.35 ATR across
the window — 3.7× out of reach at its closest, and 0 events in 501 bars. Its comment also
describes a different rule ("the compression (a zone whose height shrinks across retests)",
[events.rs:2001](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/events.rs#L2001)): the code compares a
zone's height against a volatility number, it never compares the zone against its own earlier
heights.

**6. `sd.zone_strength` is dead code, and it is the family's only `readout`.** The event is
built with its score at [events.rs:2031](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/events.rs#L2031)
and pushed into `deferred_strength`, declared at
[events.rs:2000](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/events.rs#L2000) and never read again —
it is not merged into the returned vector, so the concept cannot emit on any window. Two
things follow. The score, if it were merged, would still not be *visible*: it rides in
`magnitude`, which survives to the browser as `evidence.magnitude` (present on 1,169 of the
4,247 events on this window) and is read by neither renderer — `ConceptShape`'s only text
member prints a name derived from `concept_id`, never a measured value. And the grade itself
inverts the school it is registered in: `0.4 * min(touches/4, 1)` rewards touch count with the
largest weight in the formula, and the recency term rewards a *recent touch*, while this
family's premise is that every revisit spends the zone.

## The card is a second renderer, and it disagrees

`sd.zone_tested` is in `CONCEPT_ZONE_IDS` and `sd.proximal_distal` is not
([concept_shapes.js:27](../../../../frontend/app/concept_shapes.js#L27)), but the card branches
on payload fields ([`previewEvents`, app.js:14768](../../../../frontend/app/app.js#L14768)), so
both cards draw boxes and the chart draws a box for one and a line for the other. The card also
extends every `confirmed` box to the right edge of its own crop, while the chart stops each box
at its event bar — so the same nine `zone_tested` events are nine short bands on the chart and
nine full-width bands in the card. [pass2-cards.png](evidence/pass2-cards.png) shows all of it
at once: `Proximal / Distal Lines` previewed as one wide dark box, `Supply/Demand Zone Test` as
a band across the whole crop, and the two silent concepts as bare candlesticks under a count of
`0`.
`preview_agrees`: 2 `differs`, 2 `not_rendered`.

## No parameter is honoured

`sd.zone_tested` declares `swing_lookback` (default 10) and the detector is handed the replay's
`structure_lookback: 3`; the other three declare nothing, which is its own finding — a zone
built from swings clustered within 0.4% with a minimum of 2 touches has three tuning knobs
inside `SrZoneConfig` and exposes none. `parameters.honoured: []` on all four rows.

## Fixes this pass asks for

1. **Stop the two detectors from sharing a family name.** Either the three S/R-derived
   concepts move to the level family they are computed from, or `detect_structure_levels`
   switches to `regions::detect_zones` and the family means one thing. This is the pass's only
   *semantic* fix, and every other row below is moot until it is decided — but it is a
   detection question, and `fires.verdict` records it rather than settling it here.
2. **Carry the lifecycle across the event boundary** (`concepts.rs:2447`): `name` (the side,
   which is the label and the colour), `to`, `mitigated` and `mitigated_at`. The zone model is
   already correct; the wire is the gap. This is the cheapest large win in the audit — one
   struct, and `sd.zone_tested` becomes the reference's drawing without touching the engine.
3. **Fix the inverted `zone_low`/`zone_high`** at events.rs:2016 and let `sd.proximal_distal`
   route to the zone branch, with both edges named — PROXIMAL and DISTAL are the trader's words
   for them and the registry already says "the zone's own entry and stop lines".
4. **A `readout` painter** — the number, its window, and its host. Pass 2 defines the shape in
   [00-vocabulary.md](00-vocabulary.md); pass 11 (`indicators.zscore`) is its second customer.
5. **Merge or delete `deferred_strength`.** A registered concept that cannot emit is a broken
   promise in the library: the card shows its owner bare candlesticks under a count of `0`.
6. **Say SUPPLY or DEMAND on the chart.** `sd.zone_tested` carries the side in `direction` and
   spends it on a colour; the word that tells a trader which way the zone defends is the one
   thing the payload already has and the label still omits.
