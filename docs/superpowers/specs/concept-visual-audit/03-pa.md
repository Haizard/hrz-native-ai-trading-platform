# Pass 3 — Price Action (11 rows)

Judged 2026-10-29 against `main`. The machine-readable verdicts are the 11 rows of
[`ledger/pa.json`](../../../../reports/concept-visual-audit/ledger/pa.json);
`node tools/concept_visual_audit.mjs check --family pa` reports **11 rows, 0 incomplete**.

**Headline: this family draws consequences and forgets the event.** One fact happened in the
measured window — a close through 80,451.18 at bar 50 of 501 — and the family spends 451 marks
on it: the break itself once ([`pa.breakout`](../../../../reports/concept-visual-audit/pa/pa.breakout.html)),
the return to it 17 times (`pa.retest`, bars 51-67), and the reclaim 433 times
(`pa.failed_breakout`, bars 68-500, of which the scan's 50-event cap ships the newest 50). All
451 are at one price, all of them zero-width, and none of them connected to bar 50. That is the
pass in one sentence, and it is a *transport* failure rather than a rendering one: 285 of the
family's 335 events answer `bars: [t, t]`, because the detector's own `push` helper has no slot
for a first bar at all — its signature is `|kind, side, price|` and it fills `formed_at: None`
([events.rs:2924-2936](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/events.rs#L2924)) —
and `as_concept_event` turns a missing `formed_at` into a same-bar span
([concepts.rs:2447](../../../../crates/market-data/src/concepts.rs#L2447)), which
`position_concept_shape` floors at `bar_w * 0.6` — about 1.3px at the default zoom.

**Two of the eleven cannot fire at all, and one is a subset of another.** `pa.level_flip` is
unreachable by algebra (finding 6), `pa.compression_breakout`'s two clauses never hold on the
same bar (finding 7), and `pa.volume_confirmed_breakout` is a strict subset of `pa.breakout`
that stays silent on 20 of the 41 breaks it is named after (finding 8).

**The vocabulary held for the third pass running.** Ten of the eleven rows ask for shapes
already settled or already named (`level_run` × 6, `lifecycle_box` × 3, `time_box` × 1), and the
eleventh claims pass 8's `bar_marker` — as `wiring`, not `new_rendering`, because the painter
for a glyph at a bar already exists and the missing piece is a bar index the detector throws
away. No new geometry, and no fifth `kind`.

## What the reference set says

Six LuxAlgo Library concept pages and two docs pages carry this family; all eight were read on
2026-10-29 and every sentence below is verbatim. They agree on one structural point the render
table above contradicts: **the level is the drawing and the break is a mark on it.**

1. **The boundary comes first.** "A horizontal zone bounded by a confirmed high and low that
   price rotates inside until a breakout ends it" and "End the range when a bar closes above RH
   (upside breakout) or below RL (downside breakout)"
   ([Trading Range](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/trading-range/)) — the same page's
   calculation uses `RH = max(H)` / `RL = min(L)` over a window "commonly around 20", which is
   our `RANGE_BARS: 20` almost exactly, and it says a range is confirmed by "heavily overlapping
   candles, alternating failures at both edges, flattening moving averages, and low
   trend-strength readings all point the same way" — several readings, not one span comparison.
2. **A break is graded by acceptance, not by a single excursion.** "Look for acceptance rather
   than a single excursion: closes beyond the boundary, a retest that holds outside, and
   expansion in volatility or volume"
   ([Trading Range](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/trading-range/)). The three steps —
   break, retest, expansion — are one figure's story, which is why the reference set has no
   third mark on the chart for them and our catalogue has three concepts.
3. **A retest is judged across a band, and a flip is the same level changing role.** "A retest is
   a return to a level that price has already broken, approached now from the other side";
   "rejection wicks and a close back in the breakout direction argue the level held; a
   full-bodied close back through it converts the event into a false breakout signal instead";
   "if the old ceiling now behaves as a floor, the level has flipped, the textbook role
   reversal"; "That is also why grading uses a zone rather than a line: defense happens across a
   band of prices, so a tolerance around the level, scaled to recent volatility, separates a wick
   probing the zone from a genuine failure"
   ([Retest](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/retest/)). Note what this does to
   `pa.retest` / `pa.failed_breakout`: the reference makes them the *same* observation read at
   two bars, and it makes the tolerance a band, not a price.
4. **A failure is anchored on the re-entry and it is quick.** "Watch for the failure: price
   cannot hold beyond the level and closes back inside, often within a few bars"
   ([False Breakout](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/false-breakout/)). "Within a few
   bars" is the sentence our unbounded look-forward violates.
5. **Volume is a grade attached to a break, not a second break.** "Check participation: volume at
   breakout running meaningfully above recent norms supports the case that new business is being
   done beyond the level; a quiet break deserves more suspicion"
   ([Breakout](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/breakout/)).
6. **A grid of figures stands before price reaches it.** "Draw the grid across the visible range
   and thin it when lines crowd the chart; levels with more trailing zeros stay, finer
   subdivisions go first" and "trailing zeros generally means more attention"
   ([Round Numbers](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/round-numbers/)). Nothing is
   detected: the trader chooses a density and then grades what happens on each line.
7. **A swing label sits at the pivot, and the pivot's size is a setting.** "Swing points are
   displayed retrospectively, and as such are not detected where they are located. They should
   not be used for real-time applications"
   ([docs: Market Structures](https://docs.luxalgo.com/platform/algos/price-action-concepts/market-structures));
   the settings page exposes the sensitivity as a number — "Numerical default: 5" for internal
   structure, "Numerical default: 50" for swing — with "Higher values will return longer-term
   swing points" ([docs: settings](https://docs.luxalgo.com/platform/algos/price-action-concepts/settings)).
   Our swing is a hard-coded 3 bars either side, and the pivot's bar index is discarded.
8. **A regime is a per-bar state, and dwell time is part of the definition.** "A trend regime
   label is a discrete classification stamped on each bar: uptrend, downtrend, or range,
   sometimes with strength grades"; "require the new condition to hold for several bars, or use
   a stricter threshold to enter a state than to stay in it, so the label does not flip on every
   borderline bar"
   ([Trend Regime Label](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/trend-regime-label/)).

## What we draw today

**1. Eight of the eleven have no length, because the detector's own helper has nowhere to put
it.** `detect_price_action`'s `push` closure takes `(kind, side, price)` and fills
`zone_low: None, zone_high: None, magnitude: None, formed_at: None` for every event it emits
([events.rs:2924-2936](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/events.rs#L2924)), and the fused
block in `detect_structure_levels` passes `None` for `formed_at` on both of its concepts
([events.rs:1919](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/events.rs#L1919),
[events.rs:1955](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/events.rs#L1955)). So 285 of the 335
events on this window — every concept except `pa.level_rejection` — answer `bars: [t, t]`, and
the router's zero-length floor draws them as 1.3px stubs. The information is not missing from
the analysis: `pa.range_contraction` knows which ten bars it is tight about, `pa.retest` knows
which bar broke, `pa.regime` knows when the state began. The event type has one slot for a
second time and the family leaves it `None`.

**2. `pa.failed_breakout` is a state wearing event clothes, and it is the loudest row in the
audit so far.** The loop scans the window's channel breaks oldest first and, for the first one,
asks whether *any* later close came back on the original side — with the look-forward written as
`&candles[i + 1..]` ([events.rs:2955-2985](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/events.rs#L2955)),
which reaches the newest bar. One break therefore satisfies the predicate on every bar from the
reclaim to the end: bars 68 through 500 here, **433 of the 480 replayable bars**, and the scan's
cap shows the newest 50 of them at one price. The `break` after the first qualifying `i` also
means no newer break is ever examined, so the concept can only ever talk about one level —
measured: 50 events, 1 distinct price. The reference's "often within a few bars" is the
bounded-horizon version of this same predicate; ours has no horizon.

**3. `pa.retest` and `pa.failed_breakout` are one bar-50 break read two ways.** Same level
(80,451.18), disjoint bars (51-67 then 68-500), and the code makes them mutually exclusive per
bar — the failure clause is tested first and both branches `break` out of the scan
([events.rs:2974-2981](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/events.rs#L2974)) — so the family
presents a single historical fact as two concepts that never coexist. Neither marks the touch,
neither draws the level, and the 17-bar retest run is exactly the "return to the level" the
reference page describes, flattened into 17 dashes at the same price.

**4. `pa.higher_highs` throws away its own pivot index.** The swing collection pushes prices,
not bars — `highs.push(k.high)` ([events.rs:3010-3027](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/events.rs#L3010))
— and the event then names the *newest* swing's price on the *newest* bar. 50 events land on 6
distinct levels in runs of up to 13 consecutive bars, which is the signature of a state ("the
last two swings step up") re-emitted per bar. The reference's own words are the fix: swing
points are displayed retrospectively, at the pivot, and "should not be used for real-time
applications" — a label at today's bar over a price made 40 bars ago is precisely the real-time
misuse that sentence warns about. This is also the pass's only `wiring` row: the glyph-at-a-bar
painter exists (`CandleMark`), what is missing is the index.

**5. `pa.level_rejection` is the one concept with a run — and it draws the run at the midpoint
of a band nobody paints.** It is the only pa concept passing `formed_at: Some(zone.first_time)`
([events.rs:1877](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/events.rs#L1877)), so its 50 events span
43 / 331 / 444 bars (min / median / max) instead of zero. But the price it runs at is
`(zone.low + zone.high) / 2` — measured: `price_level` equals the zone midpoint on 50 of 50
events — while `zone_low`/`zone_high` are also on the wire and the router ignores them, because
no `pa.*` id is in `CONCEPT_ZONE_IDS`
([concept_shapes.js:27](../../../../frontend/app/concept_shapes.js#L27)). The reference grades a
rejection "across a band of prices, so a tolerance around the level"; we compute the band, ship
the band, and draw its centre line.

**6. `pa.level_flip` cannot fire, and the proof is algebra rather than measurement.** In the
same zone loop the rejection branch is tested first and `break`s
([events.rs:1873-1884](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/events.rs#L1873)), and both flip
clauses are strict subsets of it: `through_up` asks `candles[n - 1].close > hi && last.close > hi
&& last.low <= hi && last.close > hi` ([events.rs:1888](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/events.rs#L1888)),
where `candles[n - 1]` *is* `last` (`let n = candles.len() - 1`), so the clause reduces to
`last.close > hi && last.low <= hi` — and that already satisfies `wick_in && close_out`, because
`last.close > hi ≥ lo` forces `last.high ≥ lo` and the second half is the clause's own. The
pushes at [events.rs:1891](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/events.rs#L1891) and
[1895](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/events.rs#L1895) are dead code. The card therefore
shows a bare candlestick under a count of `0` on every window, and the row is
`impossible_in_vocabulary` — not because the flip cannot be drawn (the reference draws it as one
level with two roles) but because there is nothing on the chart to compare.

**7. `pa.compression_breakout`'s two clauses never meet.** Replaying the predicate bar by bar
over the window at three different replay starts (the scan's own offset, ±5 bars): the squeeze
clause (`range < 3 × mean bar range`) holds on **3** bars, the displacement clause
(`body ≥ 2 × mean body`) on 60-64, and the conjunction on **0** at every offset. The two are
measured against different denominators — the *whole window's* range against the mean of its
bars, and one candle's body against the mean of all bodies — so the "fused signal" is a ratio
that is arithmetically near-impossible rather than rare. It is `differs_major` with
`not_rendered`, and if it did fire it would draw what `pa.breakout` draws: a 1.3px dash with the
squeeze it is named for absent from the wire (`formed_at: None` at
[events.rs:1919](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/events.rs#L1919)).

**8. `pa.volume_confirmed_breakout` is a subset that deletes half the breaks and never ships
the ratio.** Measured: 21 of the 41 breaks `pa.breakout` reports, and every one of its
(bar, price) pairs is also a `pa.breakout` event — a strict subset by construction, since it is
the same 20-bar channel test with a fresh-cross guard and a 1.5× volume filter on top
([events.rs:1948-1957](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/events.rs#L1948)). Twenty breaks —
49% — happened and this concept stays silent about them, which inverts the reference's stance
that "a quiet break deserves more suspicion" is a *grading* step attached to a break, not a
second break that only speaks when the first one was loud. And the number that decided it is
`magnitude: None` on the wire, so neither renderer can show the participation it is named for.

**9. `pa.regime` is a state that ships only its transitions — 29 of them, one every ~17 bars.**
`classify` compares the tail against the tail-minus-one and fires when they differ
([regime.rs:71-80](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/regime.rs#L71)) with no dwell time, so
the window carries 29 shifts (14 neutral, 9 bearish, 6 bullish) — the borderline flip-flop the
reference page names as the thing to design against. Twenty-nine shifts describe thirty
stretches of the window and the chart receives twenty-nine zero-width points, so the state each
one announces has no drawing at all. Two details make the row worse than it looks. The event
carries `zone_low: Some(last.low), zone_high: Some(last.high)` — the shifting bar's own wick —
so the card paints a one-bar box for every shift while the chart paints a line
([regime.rs:96-99](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/regime.rs#L96)). And the ADX reading
that decided each shift does arrive, unconditionally, because the push falls back to the ADX line
when the regime has no side of its own (`magnitude.or(adx_line[...])`), so 29 numbers the trader
never sees ride on the wire — 41.41, 31.08, 25.18 in the sample. `structure_lean` is the same
two-swing HH/LL test `pa.higher_highs` reports
([regime.rs:31-46](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/regime.rs#L31)), so one family contains
the same rule twice under two names.

**10. The label is the detector's name, in snake_case.** `concept_shapes.js` derives `short`
from `concept_id.split(".")[1]` and only the `candle_mark` branch humanises it
([concept_shapes.js:47](../../../../frontend/app/concept_shapes.js#L47),
[96](../../../../frontend/app/concept_shapes.js#L96)), so the chart prints `failed_breakout`,
`range_contraction`, `volume_confirmed_breakout`, `higher_highs` — visible in
[pass3-chart.png](evidence/pass3-chart.png). Pass 1 recorded the same finding for SMC; this pass
photographs it again because the family's labels are longer and the underscore is the only thing
separating two concepts named `breakout` and `volume_confirmed_breakout`.

## The card is a second renderer, and it disagrees three ways

The card's counts are the API's counts exactly — 41, 50, 17, 50, 50, 27, 50, 0, 0, 21, 29 — so
the two renderers agree perfectly about *how much*. About *what*, they split the family three
ways, because the card branches on payload fields
([`previewEvents`, app.js:14768](../../../../frontend/app/app.js#L14768)) while the chart
branches on the registry id:

| | concepts | events | the chart | the card |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| zone fields present | level_rejection, round_numbers, volume_confirmed_breakout, regime | 127 | a line at `price_level` — no `pa.*` id is in `CONCEPT_ZONE_IDS` | a filled box |
| `price_level`, no zone fields | breakout, failed_breakout, retest, higher_highs, range_contraction | 208 | a 1.3px stub | **nothing at all** |
| silent | level_flip, compression_breakout | 0 | — | bare candlesticks under a `0` |

The middle row is the card's own zero-span problem: it draws
`moveTo(x(from == null ? at : from)) … lineTo(x(at))`, and with `from == at` the path has no
length, so a dashed line paints nothing. Sixty-two per cent of the family's events — 208 of 335,
across five cards each wearing a confident count badge — are invisible in the preview that sells
them.

The top row disagrees in the other direction. Every one of the 335 events answers
`status: "confirmed"`, so the card's `forming || confirmed` test is always true and always
extends the box to `axis.bars - 1` — the right edge of its own crop. `pa.regime` therefore shows
29 boxes of one bar's wick stretched across the whole preview; `pa.round_numbers` shows the
crossing bar's wick as a band at 80,000 while the grid step it actually used (`magnitude: 5000`,
present on all 27) is unread by both renderers; `pa.volume_confirmed_breakout` shows the 20-bar
range as a box with no start time on the wire (`from == at`), so it too runs to the edge. The
chart stops each of them at its event bar and drops the band entirely.
`preview_agrees`: 9 `differs`, 2 `not_rendered`.

## No parameter is honoured

All eleven declare `parameters: []` in the registry
([concepts.rs:2265-2395](../../../../crates/market-data/src/concepts.rs#L2265)) — the third
consecutive family where nothing is exposed. Meanwhile the family *is* a set of constants:
`RANGE_BARS 20`, `CONTRACTION_BARS 10`, `CONTRACTION_WINDOWS 10`
([events.rs:2911-2913](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/events.rs#L2911)), the 3-bar swing
on either side of a pivot, `tolerance_pct 0.004 / min_touches 2 / max_zones 6` inside
`SrZoneConfig` ([sr_zones.rs:36-44](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/sr_zones.rs#L36)), the 1.5×
volume ratio, the 2× body and 3× range clauses, ADX 25, and the round-number step
`10^floor(log10(price)) / 2` ([events.rs:2083](../../../../crates/analytics-core/src/events.rs#L2083))
— which is why the grid jumps an order of magnitude the moment price crosses 100,000. The
reference set exposes the two that matter most as user-facing numbers: the swing sensitivity
(defaults 5 and 50) and, for the box family, `Extend Imbalance` (default 10). A trader who wants
a swing structure rather than an internal one has no way to ask.

## Fixes this pass asks for

1. **Give the detectors a first bar.** `formed_at` is one argument at each `push`; with it,
   `pa.breakout` gets the 20-bar channel it broke, `pa.range_contraction` gets the ten bars it is
   tight about, `pa.failed_breakout` gets the break-to-reclaim span, and `pa.regime` gets the
   shift-to-now state. This single field moves nine of the eleven rows and touches no renderer.
2. **Bound the failure horizon and anchor it on the re-entry.** `candles[i + 1..]` → a parameter
   (the reference's "within a few bars"), and emit at the reclaim bar rather than on every bar
   after it. Without this, `pa.failed_breakout` is a state that occupies 90% of the window.
3. **Merge the break/retest/failure triad into one level with a history.** The reference reads
   them as three steps of one figure; three concepts stamping one price at three bar ranges is
   the noise the user's brief is about. At minimum, `pa.retest` should mark the touch bar.
4. **Stop drawing a band's midpoint.** `pa.level_rejection` already ships `zone_low`/`zone_high`;
   route the `pa.*` zone-carrying ids into the zone branch and label the two edges. The band is
   the reference's whole point about grading.
5. **Mark the pivot at its own bar, with the four-word vocabulary.** HH/HL/LH/LL at the pivot,
   retrospectively — the reference's exact convention, and the only `wiring` row in the pass.
   `structure_lean` and `pa.higher_highs` should then share one swing implementation.
6. **Delete or fix the two dead concepts.** `pa.level_flip`'s branch is unreachable (either the
   rejection clause stops `break`ing, or the flip is a level-role change computed on its own
   terms); `pa.compression_breakout`'s clauses need a common denominator. A registered concept
   that cannot emit shows its owner bare candlesticks under a `0`.
7. **Ship the volume ratio and stop treating it as a second break.** `magnitude` on
   `pa.volume_confirmed_breakout`, and the concept becomes the grade the reference describes —
   which also makes it able to speak about the 20 quiet breaks it currently ignores.
8. **Add dwell time to the regime classifier** — the reference's "hold for several bars, or a
   stricter threshold to enter than to stay" — and draw the state as a `time_box` with the shift
   as its left edge. 29 shifts in 501 bars is not a regime read.
9. **Humanise the segment labels** (`failed_breakout` → `Failed Breakout`), the fix pass 1 asked
   for and the one this family needs most, since its names are long and three of them share the
   word `breakout`.
